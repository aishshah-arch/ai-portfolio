Professional Bio

Who I am, where I've been, and where I'm headed.

I've spent the past eight years helping organizations make better decisions with data. Most recently, I was a Senior Quantitative Analyst at JPMorgan Chase Wealth Management on the Audience Targeting team, where I built propensity models with XGBoost and LightGBM, used SHAP to explain model decisions to non-technical stakeholders, and designed audience strategies and executive dashboards for national marketing campaigns.

Before JPMorgan, I worked in business consulting, including at AGCO and during my graduate studies at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I earned an M.S. in Information Management with a 4.0 GPA. My foundation is a B.E. in Computer Science from Bhilai Institute of Technology.

Along the way, I became especially interested in one question: how do you make sophisticated models genuinely usable by the people who rely on them? That question led me to build tools like a browser-based fair lending analyzer that lets compliance teams run rigorous statistical tests without writing code.

Today, I'm pursuing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Indiana Wesleyan University (expected April 2028), going deeper into machine learning, large language models, and prompt engineering. My goal is to grow into an AI Engineer who builds responsible, explainable AI systems for industries where accuracy and trust are non-negotiable.
